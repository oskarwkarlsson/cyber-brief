Vulns

Unit 42 sees automated exploitation of YOOtheme Pro CVE-2026-102785

The unauthenticated RCE in the YOOtheme Pro image handler (versions 5.0.0 to 5.0.52, fixed in 5.0.53 on Oct 21) was hit two days after the patch. A scanner wrote JPEG/PHP polyglot webshells (yt[0-9a-f]{7}.php in wp-content/uploads), then an operator dropped an ALFA webshell. Public PoCs exist since Oct 23. Update now and hunt for those files.

University of Copenhagen: hackers took data on 525,000 people from a test environment

Names, Danish CPR national ID numbers, phones and emails may be exposed, plus PIN codes for another 20,000 staff and students, so some campus areas were locked down while new codes go out. Detected last Tuesday, contained, reported to the Danish DPA. Expect CPR-based phishing in Denmark.

DreamHost forces email and FTP/SFTP password resets in an ongoing incident

The host (400,000+ customers) locked out users until passwords are reset and tells owners to check mailboxes for unknown forwarding rules, which hints some email accounts were accessed. File Manager is still down and DreamHost has not explained what happened. Change any reused DreamHost passwords.

Korean police: ex-pentester used ARTEX AI to breach 90+ organizations

Seoul police say a 32-year-old former pentester and six others stole 8.2M records from schools, hospitals and companies, demanding about $270,000 in 38 cases, after adopting the ARTEX AI pentest framework in late August. It is the same tool tied to the attacks on South Korean banks. Police call it Korea's first arrest for large-scale AI-assisted hacking.
