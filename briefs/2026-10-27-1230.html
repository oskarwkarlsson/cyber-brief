Vulns

Tailscale confirms Tailscale SSH privilege-escalation flaws, targets a fix by end of Oct 27

Tailscale's investigation page (published Oct 26) says most issues it found let someone already allowed to connect over Tailscale SSH go beyond their local user's permissions, potentially to elevated privileges, and calls them "serious". Fixes for most issues are in testing for a client release targeted by the end of Tuesday Oct 27. Still no CVE. Until then, tighten SSH access policies and set TS_SSH_DISABLE_FORWARDING=1 if you don't need forwarding.

Australia's ASD hacks an Amadey backend and cuts it off from 180,000 infected computers

ASD's acting deputy director-general of intelligence told ABC News the agency got into one criminal's Amadey loader controller and disconnected it from about 180,000 victims worldwide, including in Australia. The malware was not removed and earlier theft is not undone. ASD also says it broke in and destroyed 5 TB of stolen business data before it could be sold. Stolen credentials from these hosts may still surface.

ENGIE confirms theft of French customer data from a partner marketing tool

The energy giant says attackers accessed a prospecting tool used by partners, not its internal IT. Letters to customers list names, postal and email addresses, phone numbers, PDL/PCE meter points, customer IDs and contract numbers; no banking data. No group has claimed it. Expect convincing energy-contract phishing in France.

Booking software provider RMS Cloud breach fuels payment phishing at Australian holiday parks

RMS, which serves 6,000+ hotels, parks and apartments, is investigating WhatsApp, SMS and email messages that cite genuine booking details and ask for card payment. BIG4, Reflections and G'day Parks guests are affected. RMS says no card data was exposed from its systems. A classic third-party platform breach turned into targeted fraud.
