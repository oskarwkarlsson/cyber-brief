HPE patches eight maximum-severity ArubaOS (AOS-8 and AOS-10) flaws

Part of 72 fixes for the OS behind Aruba mobility conductors, controllers, gateways and access points. The eight top-rated bugs allow unauthenticated remote auth bypass and code execution with low complexity and no user interaction. HPE says it knows of no public PoC or exploitation, but "strongly urges" patching and restricting CLI and web management to a dedicated VLAN or firewalled segment.

PHP discloses another RCE, CVE-2026-115560, with no patch yet

Only exploitable with an unusual setup: session.serialize_handler set to php_binary or php_serialize, switched to php at runtime via ini_set() before session_start(), with session upload progress enabled. PHP will fix it in the regular release cycle. Mitigation is session.upload_progress.enabled=0, the same step urged before the PHP2Shell patches, so hosts that applied it then are covered.

Access broker "k0t" breached exposed FortiGate and GlobalProtect firewalls worldwide

Group-IB and NyxLab tie the campaign to k0t (aka CryptoFeel, Member19, QazWsxShop), assessed as an initial access broker or affiliate with links to Qilin and The Gentlemen. Most compromises fell between February and June 2026; victims are about 38% APAC, 32% LatAm, 16% Europe, 13% MENA. Post-access tooling includes NetExec, Impacket, noPac and Kerbrute. The Hacker News reports over 70,000 credentials exposed (not checked against the report). Review edge-device logins and rotate VPN credentials.
