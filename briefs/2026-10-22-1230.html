Cisco Expressway: critical RCE as root (CVE-2026-76466)

Part of 34 Cisco fixes across Expressway, IOS XR, Snort 3 and Unified Communications. Persistent cookies plus weak client-certificate validation in some configs let attackers reach the database RPC service and run commands as root. Fixed in Expressway X15.5.20. No exploitation reported.

PHP2Shell: mass exploitation attempts began right after the patches

Amazon says probing started Oct 17 under embargo from a commercial anonymization network, multiple PoCs from different infrastructure surfaced about 12 hours before disclosure, and mass activity followed the Oct 21 patches. Attempts seen so far were unsuccessful. Patch now if you haven't.
