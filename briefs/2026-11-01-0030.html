Hosting platform Northflank tells every customer to assume their secrets were decrypted after AI API keys were abused, and Wiz counts nine AI developer platforms breached in October

Top stories
Incidents

Northflank breach: "assume all environment variables have been decrypted", and customers report their AI API keys being used

The cloud app hosting platform got a threat alert from Wiz at 11:43 UTC on Oct 30, pushed network policy patches and brought in Wiz incident response. Its status page tells all users to rotate every secret in workloads, secret groups and global secrets, revoke keys with the issuing providers, disable public access to add-ons and check AI provider billing logs. It has added rotation for Postgres, MongoDB, MySQL, Redis and (as of 22:32 UTC Oct 31) Minio add-ons, and turned on audit logs for all accounts. Root cause and number of affected customers are not disclosed.

Wiz "Black October": nine AI developer platforms disclosed breaches this month, all hunting stored environment variables with AI keys

Wiz lists Fireworks AI, Andromeda, Zeabur, Lightning AI, OpenCode, Railway, Warp, CommandCode and Northflank. Fireworks, OpenCode and Warp report signs of an "agent swarm" attack, and OpenCode and CommandCode blame attempts at model "distillation". Wiz ties the wave to LLMJacking (reselling or burning stolen model access): in two cases it investigated, stolen enterprise credentials made about 2.5 million Amazon Bedrock calls worth about $100,000. Wiz does not claim one actor is behind all nine. Zeabur says it will shut down because of its incident. If you keep AI provider keys on any of these platforms, rotate them and watch usage.
