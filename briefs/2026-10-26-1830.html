Top stories
Vulns

CERT-UA: attackers are taking over Dahua cameras and recorders

Attackers guess predictable serial numbers to find devices on Dahua's Easy4IP cloud remote-access feature, then use weak or default passwords or the 2021 auth-bypass bugs CVE-2021-33044/33045 to change settings and add accounts. Hunt.io says backdoor accounts can survive password changes and most factory resets, so firmware updates are needed. Device counts and attribution not disclosed.

Vulns

Two critical Wasmtime flaws let malicious WebAssembly escape the sandbox

CVE-2026-115889 (Cranelift value-number overflow) and CVE-2026-115893 (ARM64 br_table jump) are rated 9.9. Risk is highest for hosts that compile untrusted modules, such as edge and serverless platforms. Fixed in 36.0.19, 48.0.7, 49.0.4 and 50.0.0; programs embedding Wasmtime must be rebuilt, and the ARM64 bug has no reviewed GitHub entry, so Dependabot will not alert. No exploitation reported.

Genesys says hackers stole customer CRM data from internal systems

The call-center software maker says attackers reached its learning management system and CRM and likely downloaded customer contact and account data (names, phones, business emails, job titles). Genesys Cloud and Engage platforms were not affected; it says the incident was contained Oct 23. ScarlettGroup claims the attack (claim, unverified). Expect Genesys-themed phishing.

Unit 42: criminals are standardizing on Hermes Agent plus DeepSeek V4

An actor called BUGSTER ran the Hermes Agent framework with DeepSeek V4 and Cursor, safety controls off, to brute-force one Indian university site and breach another, taking 5,200+ student records, plus VoIP fraud planning and Wi-Fi cracking. Unit 42 sees multiple unrelated actors using the same cheap, easily jailbroken combo.
