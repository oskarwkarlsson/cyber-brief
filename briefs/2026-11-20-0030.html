Vulns

vCenter CVE-2026-123890 was a zero-day

Mandiant/GTIG says suspected PRC-nexus ARMORY CASTLE (formerly UNC5221) exploited the vCenter auth bypass patched on Thursday (VMSA-2026-0009) in early September, using a Go tool called BONESAW to get an administrator@vsphere.local session over port 2012, dump the LMDB, steal the STS SAML signing key, create an SSO admin account and forge SAML tokens, then move to ESXi. Initial access was the BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day CVE-2026-102764. BONESAW worked against a vCenter already patched for July's CVE-2026-59309; it hits 7.x and 8.x, and 7.0 has no public fix. Takeaway: patch to 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200 or 8.0 U3m now, restrict port 2012, and hunt for new SSO admin or machine accounts.

Skagerak Energi hit after Equinor

Norwegian hydropower and grid group Skagerak Energi (owner of grid operator Lede) said on November 18 it detected activity that may be an attempted cyberattack on some IT systems; on November 19 it said it has a good overview, with no impact on operations or production and no sign of data loss, and it has moved to "yellow" readiness. Equinor said on Thursday afternoon it is still working to stop its attack, that production is normal and that the attack does not appear to target Equinor specifically. No link between the two has been confirmed; Aker BP and Statkraft have raised monitoring. Takeaway: Nordic energy IT is under active pressure; watch NSM for indicators.

TfL Oyster photocard breach, 265,000 users

Transport for London says about 265,000 Oyster photocard customers may have had account details exposed. It found the incident on November 16 after customers reported account-creation emails sent from TfL's own mail systems as part of the compromise. The photocard site is offline, cards stay valid, and notifications are going out. TfL was already hit by a major attack in 2024. Takeaway: expect convincing follow-on phishing that uses real TfL senders and account details.

Vulns

ASUS Control Center Express CVSS 10

ASUS released Control Center Express 1.7.29 fixing CVE-2026-114202 (CVSS 10), a hard-coded cryptographic key that lets an unauthenticated remote attacker forge an auth token and become administrator of the console that manages PC fleets. Two more criticals let low-privileged users forge session tokens (CVE-2026-114204) or create admin accounts (CVE-2026-114205). No exploitation reported. Takeaway: an attacker who owns the console can push to every managed PC; update and keep it off the internet.
