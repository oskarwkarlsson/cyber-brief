Top stories
Vulns

CISA KEV adds Adobe Commerce/Magento CVE-2026-48356 and Brotli CVE-2026-111497, both due Nov 10

The Adobe Commerce and Magento bug is an unrestricted file upload that can give code execution (Adobe bulletin APSB26-73). The Brotli entry is the HTTP2Shell decoder bug covered since Oct 26; its KEV listing means CISA now has evidence of exploitation, a material step up from the earlier PoC and patch-wave stage. CISA flags Brotli as a shared component, so check bundled copies in proxies, runtimes and language packages, not just the system library. Ransomware use is listed as unknown for both.

Skimmer gang hijacked Convertr's self-hosted Plausible analytics script to steal cards across Brazilian stores

Sansec says a Brazilian Magecart crew modified the Plausible tracker that loads on checkout pages of every merchant on Convertr, an e-commerce platform behind dozens of major Brazilian fashion brands, from Oct 24. By Oct 26 the skimmer was tuned to Convertr's own payment field names and took card numbers, expiry, CVV, names and CPF tax IDs. Sansec suspects (unconfirmed) an old Plausible Community Edition RCE, CVE-2026-8467, as the way in. Convertr has removed the skimmer and patched. A clean example of one shared script compromising many shops at once.

AI/Cloud

TrendAI documents a 44-agent CyberStrikeAI platform running full intrusion workflows against Japanese targets

From exposed attacker servers, TrendAI found three Simplified-Chinese-language operations hitting Japan. In Case A, the open-source CyberStrikeAI tool ran recon, exploitation, C2, persistence, exfiltration and reporting across 44 agents against at least 19 organizations (16+ in Japan, plus Taiwan and Hong Kong) in October. Case B used AI for planning and tools and also went after exposed GPU and AI inference capacity; Case C hit Yamaha RTX1210 VPNs and WebLogic. Most attempts failed, and the entry points were old exposed systems and leftover credentials.

Actors

FBI and European partners seize virtualine.net, a bulletproof host for ransomware, infostealers and scams

The Eastern District of Virginia and the FBI Washington Field Office announced the court-authorized seizure on Oct 27, with help from Germany's Polizei Hessen, Dutch police and the UK NCA. Per the Justice Department, Virtualine openly sold hosting to criminals, served ransomware operators, infostealers, carding forums and scam operations, and laundered bitcoin payments. Only the domain was seized, so expect infrastructure to shift rather than vanish.
