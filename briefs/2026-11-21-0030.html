Cairn AI agents find and exploit a zero-day on their own

Amazon Threat Intelligence found a publicly exposed Cairn instance (an open-source autonomous AI pentest framework) on September 10 and could read its operational history: prompts in Simplified Chinese, China Standard Time timestamps, targets at universities in East Asia. Given only IP addresses and the goal "obtain shell access", the agents planned and adapted on their own. Most runs failed (one ran 148 actions over 16 hours; another tried 30,000+ credential combinations), but the agents found an unpatched path traversal in an open-source medical imaging AI application, which Amazon assesses with high confidence was a zero-day, and used it to breach two universities. The maintainers have fixed it; Amazon did not name the app or victims. Takeaway: self-hosted niche AI and research apps are now realistic targets for machine-speed, around-the-clock bug hunting.

Bell Canada: "agentic" attacker stole enterprise customer data

Bell confirmed that on November 17 an "agentic threat actor" accessed several systems, including a pre-production environment of its Maximo system used to order and provision telecom circuits, and exfiltrated enterprise customer data: business contact details, ticket titles and some IP addresses of network elements. Bell says no credentials or highly sensitive personal data were in the set and customer services were not affected. It is sharing IOCs with enterprise customers and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security but has not published them. Takeaway: if you buy Bell enterprise connectivity, expect targeted phishing using real circuit and ticket details.

SAP NetWeaver Enterprise Portal flaw now on KEV

CISA added CVE-2025-42884 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 20: an unauthenticated attacker can inject JNDI environment properties or a lookup URL in SAP NetWeaver Enterprise Portal to disclose or modify server information. The due date for US federal agencies is November 23. The same release added Apache Roller CVE-2026-129448 (a broader-version CVE for the already exploited XML-RPC RCE), plus the vCenter and Cisco Expressway bugs already covered here. Takeaway: SAP portals facing the internet should get the 2025 fix now.

West Virginia Legislature hit by cyberattack

An attack that started Thursday and continued through Friday took down the West Virginia Legislature's website and forced it to disable some IT systems. Officials say it appears limited to the legislature and no other state agencies are affected; they did not say whether it is ransomware, and no group has claimed it yet. Takeaway: another US state or local government in a busy quarter for ransomware on public bodies.
