Vulns

A custom PHP2Shell exploit chained with the Fragnesia Linux bug rooted 25 firewall servers, including a US government-affiliated network

Dream says recovered attacker artifacts show a non-public exploit for PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618/109619) chained with Fragnesia (CVE-2026-46300) to get unauthenticated root on an unnamed vendor's firewalls running PHP 8.0.30. The crew fingerprinted about 19,000 exposed management interfaces, narrowed to 240 in the US, Japan, Taiwan and South Korea, rooted 24 firewalls on Oct 23-24, then went after Active Directory. Dream assesses the crew as China-nexus (not confirmed). CISA added Fragnesia to KEV with an unusually short Oct 31 deadline. Eclypsium's InfraTrust report adds the wider point: many firewalls, VPNs and NAS boxes compile PHP into firmware, so you must wait for vendor updates; keep those admin interfaces off the internet.

Volt Typhoon's covert "Silent Train" relay network ran four zero-day campaigns, and Talos ties it to new Cisco SD-WAN malware

Lumen's Black Lotus Labs says it has quietly disrupted Silent Train since late 2023: rotating chains of hijacked SOHO, IoT and enterprise devices used for zero-day exploitation of Versa Director, Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN (CVE-2026-20127) and VeloCloud Orchestrator, with hundreds of critical-infrastructure victims notified. Separately, Cisco Talos describes UAT-11473, which exploited the same SD-WAN auth bypass and planted "KeyDrift", a memory-resident framework that sits inside SD-WAN control-plane processes to steal keying material. Targets include space and satellite, defense and aerospace, and policy research, and Talos says with high confidence the infrastructure overlaps Silent Train. Patch and log SD-WAN and other management planes.

Breach wave across East Asia: Sony's ISP leaks 2.5 million records, JAL hit via Amadeus, and Korean brokers and Bithumb report attacks

Sony Network Communications says its So-net member database was accessed on Oct 19, exposing 2,585,481 records (including 443,495 NTT Docomo Hikari customers) plus 54,401 email addresses with passwords; change those passwords. Japan Airlines says hackers reached Amadeus reservation data on about 92,000 customers, with passport numbers for about 2,000. In South Korea, Daishin Securities says data on 38,490 customers was exposed, while Mirae Asset and crypto exchange Bithumb reported incidents without confirming data loss. No link between the incidents has been established. Japan is preparing emergency measures and Korea's regulator wants security checks across dozens of private IT systems.

Vulns

A 16-year-old UEFI flaw (CVE-2026-97631, CVSS 9.5) can run code before Secure Boot on network-booting servers, with no vendor firmware yet

A buffer overflow in EDK II's PXE driver lets one crafted ICMP error, sent while a machine boots over the network, hang the boot or, with a payload tuned to the firmware build, run ring-0 code before Secure Boot and measured boot. Most servers that PXE-boot are affected; disk-only machines are not. The fix is merged upstream and in Microsoft's Project Mu, but no stable EDK II release or vendor BIOS has it, and Red Hat and SUSE rate it adjacent-network only. No exploitation reported. Disable PXE where unused and watch for BIOS updates.
