Java4Shell moves from PoC to exploitation attempts

GreyNoise logged 150+ requests shaped like Java4Shell (CVE-2026-114938) attacks from five IPs, watchTowr sees "java4shell-recon" spraying plus blind exploit attempts, and Previdian logged nine attempts from six IPs; no confirmed compromises yet. VulnCheck has a full RCE exploit for customers. Cisco lists 12+ affected products with no fix or workaround yet, Red Hat rates it Critical (RHEL 8-10, vulnerable out of the box on AVX-512 VBMI CPUs), Elasticsearch 7.17.3+ is affected, and Atlassian advises disabling AVX-512 on Data Center products until patches ship. Takeaway: patch JDKs on x86 servers now, prioritise internet-facing Jira, Confluence, Jenkins and Keycloak.

NGINX2Root: public PoCs for the HTTP/2 RCE

F5's NGINX fixes now cover five critical "NGINX2Root" bugs (CVSS 9.2): the two HTTP/2 flaws already flagged plus mail proxy (CVE-2026-113425), HTTP/3 (CVE-2026-121810) and SSI/mirror proxy (CVE-2026-123634) cases. Rapid7 published a PoC for CVE-2026-123007 that defeats ASLR but needs about 128 GB of HTTP/2 traffic; Censys says it reproduced the bug over cleartext HTTP/2 without that volume, published its PoC, and counts about 9 million exposed NGINX services on affected versions with HTTP/2 on. No in-the-wild exploitation reported. Takeaway: upgrade to 1.30.7/1.31.9 (Plus 37.0.8.1/37.3.0.1) or disable HTTP/2 on exposed listeners today.

N-able Mail Assure and SpamExperts server breached

N-able says an outage that began November 16 was a security incident: attackers gained unauthorized access to a server in the Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud. Management portals, APIs, quarantine and logging remain offline in the Americas, APAC and Europe while mail filtering keeps running. N-able says it has no evidence customer data or tenant mailboxes were accessed, has not named an attacker, and says this is unrelated to the N-central bugs. Takeaway: if your MSP uses these services, ask what the partner notice said and watch for spoofed quarantine or password-reset emails.

Ruby: three critical regex and string bugs with RCE PoCs

Ruby 4.0.10, 3.4.13 and 3.3.14 fix seven memory-safety flaws in the regex engine and string case conversion; maintainers say they know of PoCs showing remote code execution for five of them (three rated critical). Exposure depends on apps compiling attacker-controlled regexes or case-converting crafted strings in single-byte encodings. No attacks reported, no CVEs assigned yet (track by GHSA id), and Ruby 3.2 and older get no fix. Four findings are credited to Ada Logics and Anthropic. Takeaway: update the interpreter itself in servers, workers and container images; updating gems is not enough.
