Apache Roller pre-auth RCE (CVE-2026-129448, CVSS 9.8) exploited in the wild

Apache's 17 November advisory says Roller's XML-RPC endpoint deserializes vendor extension types before authenticating the requester, giving unauthenticated attackers code execution on versions 4.0.0 through 6.1.4. It is the same bug as CVE-2026-82384, which had listed only 6.1.5 as affected, so many older installs may have assumed they were safe. Apache says it is actively exploited but gave no details on attackers or victim numbers. Not in CISA KEV at check time. Takeaway: upgrade to 6.1.6 or later; otherwise set enabledForExtensions to false in WEB-INF/web.xml or remove the /roller-services/xmlrpc mapping and restart, since turning XML-RPC off in the admin UI is not enough.

COLD RELIC (ex-COLDRIVER, Star Blizzard) chains one cloud breach into the next

Mandiant and GTIG say that in late 2025 the Russian espionage group took over a US organization's Google Workspace with stolen Super Admin credentials plus MFA push fatigue via a nearby residential proxy, added rogue MFA devices, passkeys and backup codes, exported the whole drive and staff directory, and tampered with Alert Center notifications for months. In 2026 it impersonated an employee of that victim over an encrypted messaging app to phish Entra ID credentials at a second US organization (Entra as SSO for Workspace), registered a new MFA device and bulk-downloaded files. Neither victim is named; GTIG saw new phishing as recently as November. Takeaway: alert on new MFA device or passkey registrations, Takeout exports and Alert Center changes, and verify "colleague" contacts arriving on messaging apps.
