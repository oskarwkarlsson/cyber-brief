Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator: SQL injection zero-day exploited (CVE-2026-114625, CVSS 9.9)

Any logged-in user, down to the lowest Enterprise Read Only customer role, can gain operator (superuser) control of the SD-WAN orchestrator and possibly the host. Arista says it is exploited but not since when or against whom. On-prem fixes: VCO 5.2.3.18, 6.1.3.10, 6.4.2.12; hosted orchestrators already patched. Releases that fixed the July and September VCO zero-days are still affected. Gateways and Edges are not.

Capcom confirms CAPCOM ID breach exposing 11.7 million accounts

Exposed: emails, account creation and last-login dates, login counts and last-login IPs; birth dates and other profile data for 43 accounts. Attackers changed passwords on 196 accounts to lock owners out (since reset). Capcom says other passwords and card data were not exposed. Detected Oct 19, reported to police and Japan's PPC on Oct 21; initial access not disclosed. Expect targeted phishing.

Two CVSS 9.0 RCE flaws in Linux KSMBD disclosed before any kernel fix

ZDI reports (found by Trend Micro's AI scanner) went to public kernel lists. ZDI-CAN-33774 is pre-auth: a race lets SMB1 data hit an SMB2 handler on any KSMBD server on port 445. ZDI-CAN-33549 needs SMB Direct over RDMA and a writable share. No mainline fix or CVE yet. Check with lsmod | grep ksmbd and disable or firewall 445 if you don't need it. RHEL doesn't ship KSMBD by default.
