Vulns

CISA adds UnrealIRCd CVE-2026-114713 to KEV: any ordinary user on a linked network can run commands with server authority

The flaw (improper neutralization of delimiters in the labeled-response module) lets a normal user inject server-to-server commands, so they can KILL, GLINE and more. UnrealIRCd's maintainer calls it the most critical issue in the project in 15 years, says it was found in an AI-assisted audit, and reports exploitation in the wild since 8 Nov. Fixes: hot-patch bundle-20261107 (6.0.0 to 6.2.7, no restart) or 6.2.7.1; a one-line SPAMFILTER workaround protects networks on 6.0.4+; EOL UnrealIRCd 5 needs the labeled-response module blacklisted. CISA added it on 13 Nov with a 16 Nov federal due date and forensic triage required. Takeaway: niche software, but if you or a community you run uses UnrealIRCd, patch now and check for rogue opers or bans.

AI/Cloud

FBI flash alert on ARTEX, as Amazon shows Chinese-nexus groups running self-funding AI-key theft and deepfake KYC bypass with it

The FBI says the open-source agentic pentest framework automates intrusions "at machine speed", with some going from targeting to privileged access in under three hours, and keeps spreading through forks and a commercial version despite the original repo's removal (Censys counts 1,600+ exposed consoles). Amazon's MadPot decoys, posing as LLM inference APIs, captured ARTEX agents' own campaign records from 3 Oct to 5 Nov: agents hunted exposed LLM gateways and AI app builders (One API, New API, LiteLLM, Dify, Langflow, Open WebUI, Ollama and others), pulled stored provider keys, validated them and fed them back to power more hunting; another campaign targeted crypto exchanges' KYC with real-time deepfake injection. Amazon attributes it with moderate confidence to disparate Chinese-nexus groups. Takeaway: any internet-facing AI gateway holding provider keys is a prime target; lock down admin panels, rotate keys and set spend alerts.

Vectors

ArcticRAT: Android crypto stealer turns into a full RAT that pipes stolen SMS to an LLM to find OTPs and balances

Zimperium zLabs says the malware, first aimed at 11 crypto apps, now offers hidden VNC behind a black "device off" overlay, keylogging, screen streaming, mic monitoring, SMS, contact and call-log theft and lock-screen credential changes (129 commands, 78 samples since July). A remotely triggered module sends installed-app lists and SMS to xAI's Grok API to pick out banking, crypto and trading apps, one-time codes and transactions and estimate a victim's wealth. Delivery is via malvertising, smishing and fake Google Play pages, with some payloads on AWS buckets; no actor attribution. Single-vendor research, IOCs published. Takeaway: never sideload "Play" apps from links, and treat Accessibility permission requests as a red flag.
