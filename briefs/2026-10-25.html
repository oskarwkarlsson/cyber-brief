Top stories
Exploited, KEV

CISA adds exploited Collabora CODE Server (CVE-2026-110447) and Arista VeloCloud (CVE-2026-114625) flaws to KEV

Both were added Friday with a federal due date of Monday, Oct 26. The Collabora bug is an OS command injection in the Nextcloud app that launches Collabora's built-in CODE server, and admins report finding PHP web shells in apps/richdocumentscode. The VeloCloud Orchestrator SQL injection (CVSS 9.9) lets any logged-in user take superuser control. Patch both, and hunt for leftover web shells, since updating won't remove a backdoor.

AI attacks

AI agents are running real intrusions end to end, cheaply

Gambit Security says a Chinese-speaking attacker used three AI harnesses over five days in September to hit 105 e-commerce sites, compromising at least 27, stealing 600,000+ card records from two and planting skimmers on five, at about $25 per completed target. Unit 42 separately saw a PHP2Shell intrusion where agentic AI tools did post-exploitation and reached MFA secrets within two hours, and SOCRadar found INC and The Gentlemen affiliates driving attacks through OpenCode with a human approving each step.

Actors

BlackX bootkit, linked to APT41, beats Secure Boot on patched Windows that lack revocations

Kaspersky says the BlackLotus successor hit diplomatic targets in Tajikistan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan in early 2026, abusing Baton Drop (CVE-2022-21894) where Microsoft's manual DBX revocation steps were never applied. It needs admin or physical access, so it is a persistence tool. Check that boot manager revocations are actually in place on your fleet.

DeFi exploit

X100 governance hijacked with a flash loan; about $4.85M in USDT drained

On Saturday an attacker used a flash loan to take two of three Senate seats, then proposed, voted and executed a treasury sale into their own empty pool in one transaction. Root cause: no balance snapshot, no stake age and no timelock. Blockaid flagged it live and counts about $4.85M realized across three transactions; Chainalysis puts it at about $4.7M.
