Microsoft sees an active phishing campaign posing as Microsoft 365 outage notices, telling users their held mail will be deleted within 24 hours unless they click

Top stories
Incidents

Phishers ride the 12 November Microsoft 365 outage with fake "Service Health" emails citing the real incident EX1550543

Microsoft Threat Intelligence says emails detected on 13 November claim messages were held during the outage and will be deleted within 24 hours unless recipients click to restore them. Subjects follow "Outage - [six-character code]", sender names use organization-specific prefixes plus "Outage Notification" or "Service Health". Sender domains: moments-notice[.]com, planetmaison[.]com, newbooker[.]com; recipient-specific SendGrid click-tracking links redirect to reallifeaccounting[.]com (path /themelist0.html). Targets span multiple US sectors. Microsoft says it is consistent with credential phishing, but credential collection and malware delivery are not yet confirmed. This turns the NCSC UK's general warning from Friday into an observed campaign. Takeaway: block the four domains, hunt EmailEvents, EmailUrlInfo and UrlClickEvents for clicks, and tell staff to check outages only in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
