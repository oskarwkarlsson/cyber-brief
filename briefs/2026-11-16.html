Incidents

La Poste subsidiary Maileva confirms a mid-September breach exposed 22 million people, about a third of France

Maileva, the Docaposte business that prints and sends payslips, invoices and registered letters for over 70,000 business users, told AFP on Saturday evening that first names, surnames and postal addresses of 22 million recipients of its clients' mail were exposed; bank details were not, it says. Clients including social landlords, medical labs and Foch hospital (via debt collector Kiwee.care) warned customers earlier this month that some letter contents, such as invoice amounts and care dates, may have been read. The Paris prosecutor confirmed on Sunday that the cybercrime brigade (BL2C) is investigating; no attacker named. Takeaway: expect convincing fake letters and invoices in France; verify any payment request through another channel.

Incidents

TSMC breach: seller 'SmithBianl' auctions 46,000 employee records from 3 BTC, with a free-leak deadline of 15 November

New detail on the breach TSMC confirmed on Friday: BleepingComputer, citing KELA, says the actor posted a 499-row sample on a hacker forum on 8 Nov from a claimed 6.9GB set with national ID numbers, addresses, contact and banking details, and threatened to publish it free if unsold by 15 Nov. TSMC's SEC filing says the data matches what a cloud service provider held for it, with no sign its own systems were compromised; it declined to say whether it engaged the seller. Whether the dump happened is not yet confirmed. Takeaway: another big firm hit through a third-party cloud host; ask your providers how employee HR data is stored and monitored.

AI/Cloud

FBI's ARTEX alert, in detail: 1,600+ exposed consoles, a paid 'ARTEX PRO' edition, and a link to the ConnectWise breach of Microsoft 365 tenants

The 13-page FLASH says multiple actors have used the AI pentest framework since at least August against finance, government, defence, tech and healthcare, with some intrusions reaching privileged access in under three hours; it plugs into Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, GLM, Grok and Kimi and favours overlooked partner portals, legacy services and third-party integrations. The original repo was pulled on 8 Oct but forks, archives, localized versions and a commercial ARTEX PRO remain, and over 1,600 consoles were exposed as of early November. MalwareMustDie released an open-source ARTEX detection signature pack and playbook. Takeaway: load the FBI IOCs and the detection pack, and lock down partner portals and cloud admin interfaces.

Vulns

Today is CISA's deadline for UnrealIRCd CVE-2026-114713 and Check Point CVE-2026-123039; no new KEV entries since Friday

Both were added to KEV on 13 Nov with a 16 Nov due date: the UnrealIRCd bug lets any ordinary user on a linked network inject server commands (exploited since 8 Nov; fix with 6.2.7.1 or hot-patch bundle-20261107), and the Check Point Gaia DynamicCLI root escalation was used in the PHP2Shell gateway intrusions that ended in an ESXi wiper. The KEV catalog has not changed since its 13 Nov release, and no new emergency patches surfaced over the weekend. Takeaway: if either is still unpatched, fix it today and hunt for rogue opers or unexpected root activity.
