Vulns

Ivanti Connect Secure CVE-2026-118527 and CVE-2026-111357 added to CISA KEV

CISA added both on Oct 30. CVE-2026-118527 (predictable identifiers) lets a remote authenticated attacker upload attacker-controlled files to a known location on disk; CVE-2026-111357 (missing authentication for a critical function) lets a local authenticated attacker escalate privileges. CISA says both chain with CVE-2026-104935, the Connect Secure auth bypass added to KEV on Oct 13, which suggests a full chain from unauthenticated access to root. Federal due dates are Nov 2 for CVE-2026-111357, with CISA's forensic triage required, and Nov 13 for CVE-2026-118527. Ransomware use: unknown. Ivanti's advisory was not opened directly; patch per Ivanti's CVE-2026-104935 advisory and hunt for compromise, not just patch.

WordPress core 4.7 to 7.1.3 upgrade-window RCE: about 63,000 attack attempts in 24 hours

WordPress 7.1.4 (Oct 28) fixes a race condition with no CVE yet (CVSS 8.1): during a full-package core update, files are unpacked to a predictable, often web-accessible wp-content/upgrade directory that includes the installer, so an unauthenticated attacker who hits it in a window of seconds can reach PHP code execution. Wordfence says it blocked 62,969 attempts in the past day, with attackers repeatedly polling paths like setup-config.php. No confirmed successful compromise reported yet. Update to 7.1.4 and block web access to wp-content/upgrade.

Hardware supply chain: Trezor warns CryptoBilis buyers after a report of a cellular implant that leaks the recovery phrase

A customer in Indonesia reported a Trezor Safe 5 bought from reseller CryptoBilis via Tokopedia with an extra board between the main board and screen, carrying a cellular module and antenna, that reads the screen including the wallet backup and sends it out when plugged in. Trezor has not examined the device yet but judged the report credible enough to tell everyone who bought any Trezor from CryptoBilis since March 2026 to move funds to a new wallet with a new recovery phrase. No losses reported to Trezor so far; no other resellers implicated. Unconfirmed pending lab analysis; reported by Trezor's blog via THN and BleepingComputer.

Incidents

Florida financial aid site breach exposes SSNs of nearly 5 million applicants

Florida's Department of Education says an unauthorized party accessed the Florida Student Financial Aid website, discovered Oct 19, exposing names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, genders, Social Security numbers and dates of birth for about 4.9 million people. The site is down; the state says the incident is contained and aid is not affected. No attacker or method disclosed. It follows California's education department breach a week earlier.
