Crypto

Binance loses roughly $6 billion in what would be the largest crypto theft on record, and Chainalysis points at North Korea

Binance paused deposits and withdrawals on Oct 29 and says its initial investigation suggests a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop gave the attacker access to a service container, which was used to submit unauthorized withdrawal transactions; it says it found no evidence of a wallet private key leak and will cover all losses. Loss figures differ (Arkham-based $5.8B at BleepingComputer, Chainalysis $5.9B at time of theft and later $6.4B), far above Bybit's roughly $1.5B in 2025. About $3B has been frozen, including 1.567B USDT by Tether and about $800M USDC by Circle (ZachXBT tally). Chainalysis says it has a high degree of confidence DPRK is responsible because the funds were commingled with proceeds of last month's Bitget exploit; ZachXBT points to TraderTraitor. Attribution is not officially confirmed. As of 04:36 UTC deposits were back, while withdrawals stay paused with resumption expected within 48 hours.

Polymarket drained through a compromised third-party monitoring script; DebugBear tells all RUM customers to pull its embed

Polymarket went cancel-only and halted trading for about an hour on Oct 29 after a third-party provider was compromised and served crypto-draining JavaScript to its users; it says affected traders will be made whole. Hudson Rock traced the malicious script to cdn.debugbear[.]com, the real-user-monitoring service DebugBear, and BleepingComputer followed about $2.5M to an attacker wallet. DebugBear confirms an ongoing incident and tells customers to remove its embed code. TechCrunch says it appears unrelated to Binance. If you load DebugBear RUM, remove it now, and treat any third-party script on pages that handle money or sessions as a supply-chain risk (subresource integrity, tight CSP).

Vulns

Edge devices under fire: Cisco ASA zero-day and GitLab join KEV with a Nov 1 deadline, and PHP2Shell is confirmed on PAN-OS

CISA added the exploited Cisco ASA/FTD SSL VPN crash bug CVE-2026-76418 (CVSS 8.6, unauthenticated, only tunnel groups with secondary auth) and GitLab CE/EE CVE-2026-97229 (CVSS 10) to KEV, both due Nov 1. Alpine Linux nearly lost its GitLab to the latter and warns that self-built GitLab on stock Ruby stays exploitable until Ruby is updated too. Palo Alto now marks PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618/109619) as ATTACKED on PA-Series, VM-Series and Panorama management interfaces, with one 11.1.7 hotfix due today, and GreyNoise has seen at least six successful exploitations. Restrict management interfaces to a jump host.

Vulns

Linux kernel CVE-2026-98432 (CVSS 9.8): remote root via a TCP handshake race, still unpatched on Ubuntu and RHEL

A use-after-free in IPv4 TCP connection setup dating back to Linux 4.4. Anthropic says its model wrote an exploit giving an unauthenticated remote attacker root on servers with a multipath TCP listener. Upstream fixed it Oct 5 and Debian, Oracle Linux, SUSE and Amazon Linux have fixed packages for at least some kernels, but Ubuntu kernels are still vulnerable or in progress and Red Hat has deferred its fix with no date. Vendor scores vary (5.9 to 8.1 outside Ubuntu). No exploitation in the wild reported; IPv6 is not affected.
