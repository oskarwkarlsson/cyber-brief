Gorbag: a smash-and-grab stealer crew that may be selling access to other Russian groups

ESET says the group has hit only Ukrainian targets since February 2025: about a quarter in law enforcement and justice, a quarter city councils near the front line, and the rest defense entities plus a major transit hub for foreign military aid. Spearphishing, sometimes from hijacked local-government mailboxes, moved in early 2026 from macro-laden Excel files to RAR archives exploiting WinRAR path traversal CVE-2025-8088 (patched July 2025). Its BeautyStealer (the GIFTEDCROOK family CERT-UA ties to UAC-0226) grabs browser passwords and cookies, OpenVPN configs, KeePass files, RDP and .pfx files and Ukrainian e-signature keys in one pass, then deletes itself. ESET thinks Gorbag may be an initial access broker for other Russia-aligned actors. One reverse shell even carried a comment telling AI reviewers the file was harmless. Takeaway: WinRAR sits outside most central patching, so check versions.
