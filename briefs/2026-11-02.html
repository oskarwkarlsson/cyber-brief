FBI attributes the Binance theft of more than $6 billion to North Korea's TraderTraitor and asks the industry to block 628 addresses

In an alert dated Oct 31, the FBI says North Korea was behind the theft "on or about October 29" and that it, the Secret Service and IRS-CI see the actors converting the haul mainly into Ether ahead of DeFi and instant swap laundering. It asks THORChain node operators, RPC providers, swap services and exchanges to block anything touching the listed addresses (608 Ethereum, 19 Bitcoin, one Solana, per BleepingComputer). This makes official what Chainalysis and TRM Labs said last week. A claim by The Hacker News that $2.85 billion has been frozen is not in the FBI alert and remains unconfirmed. If you run a VASP or DeFi front end, load the address list now.

Alpine Linux wipes and rebuilds its GitLab after near-miss exploitation of CVE-2026-97229, the Ruby regex RCE now on CISA KEV

An attacker sent malicious CI configs on Oct 20 and 21, crashing GitLab workers eight times, with four partly successful. Alpine had installed GitLab 19.3.4, and its later 19.3.6 upgrade still left it exposed because the fix lives in GitLab's bundled Ruby, which Alpine's custom container image did not include. The attacker also downloaded Alpine's GitLab container image. Alpine found no tampering in packages, releases or aports commits checked against records outside GitLab, and signing keys were never on that server, but missing logs mean it cannot rule out code execution, so it reset all passwords. Lesson: if you build GitLab or anything that compiles untrusted regexes on your own Ruby, check that Ruby is patched, not just the app version.

Vulns

Recap: CVSS 10 LXD and MicroCloud auth bypass lets anyone who can reach the API pose as a trusted client

CVE-2026-112816: LXD checked every certificate a client sent against its trust store, not just the leaf, so slipping in a trusted client's public certificate is enough to be accepted as that client. Canonical says that can mean full cluster and VM access, and root on the host depending on the victim's rights. Fixed in LXD 4.0.15, 5.0.11, 5.21.9 and 6.10.1 and MicroCloud 2.1.5. No exploitation reported. First flagged in Sunday's 12:30 alert.

Chainalysis says North Korea-linked launderers began moving funds onto Solana after emptying roughly $500 million from TRON staging wallets, with its graph showing activity across several chains.
