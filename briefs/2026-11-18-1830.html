Vulns

NGINX HTTP/2 RCE pair: CVE-2026-123633 and CVE-2026-123007 (CVSS 4.0 9.2)

F5 released nginx 1.30.7 stable and 1.31.9 mainline today, fixing a use-after-free in HTTP/2 (CVE-2026-123633) and memory corruption in HTTP/2 flow control (CVE-2026-123007, vulnerable 1.9.5 to 1.31.8), plus several medium bugs in HTTP/3, the resolver and the mail proxy. Wiz, Ikotas Labs, Metnir, Rapid7 and Xint found them independently; Wiz says both give unauthenticated code execution when HTTP/2 is enabled, and it got RCE on an NGINX serving only static files, with no backend or third-party modules. No exploitation reported yet, but with multiple teams holding working exploits, expect fast weaponisation. Takeaway: upgrade to 1.30.7 or 1.31.9 (and watch for vendor builds such as NGINX Plus, ingress controllers and distro packages); if you can't patch quickly, consider disabling HTTP/2 on exposed listeners.

Gentlemen ransomware lists NVIDIA (claim, unverified)

The Gentlemen group has added NVIDIA to its leak site, per ransomware.live and a vx-underground post. NVIDIA has not commented, and there is no word on what was taken or whether data was published. Single-source leak-site claim; treat with caution until NVIDIA or reporters confirm. Takeaway: if you exchange data with NVIDIA (partners, OEMs, cloud and design suppliers), watch for phishing that uses the news and for confirmation from NVIDIA.
