Incidents

Chainalysis says an outside actor using "offensive agentic techniques" got into its internal tools and saw customer names, emails and role labels

The blockchain analytics firm used by law enforcement, exchanges and banks disclosed on Oct 30 (US time) that it detected the intrusion earlier this month. It says it found no evidence that investigation content, credentials or session tokens were accessed, and that the incident is unrelated to the Binance theft. No technical detail yet on the flaws used or dwell time. Customers should turn on 2FA and expect phishing posing as Chainalysis, including fake "fund recovery" and recruiting pitches.

AI/Cloud

"Black October": Wiz counts nine AI developer platforms breached this month, and Northflank tells all customers to assume their secrets were decrypted

Wiz lists Fireworks AI, Andromeda, Zeabur, Lightning AI, OpenCode, Railway, Warp, CommandCode and Northflank, all hunting stored environment variables holding AI provider keys. Several report signs of an "agent swarm", and Wiz ties the wave to LLMJacking, with stolen credentials in two of its cases making about 2.5 million Amazon Bedrock calls worth about $100,000. It does not claim one actor is behind all nine. Northflank says to rotate every secret, revoke keys at the provider and check AI billing logs; Zeabur says it will shut down. If you keep AI keys on any of these platforms, rotate them now.

Vulns

Ivanti Connect Secure: two more exploited bugs on CISA KEV complete a chain from no login to root, with the first federal deadline on Nov 2

CISA added CVE-2026-111357 (privilege escalation, CVSS 8.8) and CVE-2026-118527 (file upload to a predictable location, CVSS 4.3) on Oct 30. Chained with the auth bypass CVE-2026-104935 (KEV since Oct 13), they give unauthenticated root, a chain Volexity first tied to a new actor it calls UTA0576, which used it to pull credentials, Active Directory data, admin passwords and BitLocker recovery keys rather than drop web shells. Fixes are in 25.1.1.3 and 22.8R2.6. Patch and hunt for compromise; CISA requires forensic triage for the Nov 2 item.

Vectors

A trojanized Claude Code 2.1.313 build in Cursor's extension marketplace planted an SSH key, persistence and a beacon on up to 5,000 machines

Cursor says the modified extension, not built by Anthropic, was live for about 11 hours on Oct 29. It adds an attacker SSH key (comment svc-telemetry), tries to enable SSH and open a reverse tunnel, installs persistence and beacons out; Wired reports a bug kept it from fully installing on most machines. Microsoft has published its own analysis. How the attacker published under the real extension identity is still unexplained. If affected: run Cursor's remediation script (uninstalling is not enough), remove the key and rotate credentials from a clean machine.
