Top stories
Zero-day

Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator SQL injection zero-day is being exploited (CVE-2026-114625, CVSS 9.9)

Any logged-in user, even the lowest read-only customer role, can take operator (superuser) control of the SD-WAN orchestrator. Arista confirms exploitation but not by whom. On-prem fixes: VCO 5.2.3.18, 6.1.3.10, 6.4.2.12; hosted orchestrators are patched. Builds that fixed the July and September VCO zero-days are still affected.

Breach

Railway: intruder may have read decrypted customer environment variables

The hosting platform says a third party reached systems storing customer env vars, sometimes decrypted (API keys, database and registry credentials), as part of attacks on platform companies aimed at AI inference providers. Every Railway user is told to rotate every stored secret.

Railway disclosure →
Breach

Capcom confirms CAPCOM ID breach exposing 11.7 million accounts

Emails, login metadata and last-login IPs exposed; 196 accounts had passwords changed to lock owners out (since reset). Capcom says other passwords and card data were not exposed. Detected Oct 19 and reported to police and Japan's PPC; initial access not disclosed. Expect targeted phishing.

Capcom notice →
AI attacks

JPCERT: AI pentest platforms (Cairn, CyberStrikeAI, ARTEX) used against Japanese organisations

An update to JPCERT's alert on the wave of intrusions and PII leaks in Japan since about September adds a new case: attackers using AI-driven pentest platforms to probe internet-facing web apps, steal config and backup files, and hit exposed WSUS servers. Nine suspicious source IPs (Sep 23 to Oct 20) are listed for log checks. ARTEX is the same agent Wiz ties to the Storm-0940 overlap.
