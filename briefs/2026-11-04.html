Top stories
Crypto

Binance: the $6.3 billion theft was a months-long North Korean campaign

Binance's post-mortem says it started in June when an engineer installed malware posing as a popular AI coding tool, then moved through fake job interviews, malicious downloads, compromised internal AI tooling and an advanced transaction-authorisation method. Binance says it blocked 57 other North Korean attempts this year; North Korea denies it. Chainalysis says the fake-interview playbook was refined over dozens of earlier attacks. Takeaway: developer tool installers and recruiter contact are now the main way in for TraderTraitor-style crews.

Incidents

ARTEX AI campaign: Chainstack and WorkOS confirm breaches

Blockchain node provider Chainstack says an attacker got in through a third-party component on October 26, reached production on October 29 and was cut off October 31, taking customer names, emails and password hashes; node credentials are treated as exposed. Chainstack ties it to the AI-powered campaign reported in late October. WorkOS says the ARTEX AI pentest suite was used to reach an internal sales app holding customer contact records and call summaries. Takeaway: Chainstack customers should rotate node credentials and API keys now, and WorkOS customers should expect targeted phishing.

cPGuard update channel pushed a backdoor to 2,946 Linux servers

Using stolen credentials, an attacker shipped malicious cPGuard 5.88.12 to 5.88.19 through the vendor's update CDN for under 12 hours on November 2, grabbing password hashes, root database configs, shell history and SSH keys. OpsShield says its billing database was copied too. Takeaway: update to 5.88.25 or later, then rotate every credential and key on affected hosts; the update removes the backdoor but not what was stolen.

Vulns

WordPress core race condition now in KEV, Node-RED CVSS 10 bug exploited

CISA added WordPress core CVE-2026-119977 (an upgrade-window race that lets unauthenticated attackers run code, versions 4.7 to 7.1.3) to KEV with a November 6 deadline. Separately, VulnCheck saw Node-RED CVE-2026-58115, missing authentication on the HTTP interface, exploited since October 30 to drop Linux bots and webshells. Takeaway: confirm sites are on WordPress 7.1.4 and check recent upgrades for stray files; lock down any internet-facing Node-RED without adminAuth.
