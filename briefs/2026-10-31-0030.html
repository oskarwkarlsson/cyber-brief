Revolve 8-K: attacker pulled names, contact details and saved card numbers and expiry dates "through automated means"

Revolve Group filed an 8-K on Oct 30 saying it found unauthorized access to some of its systems, remediated it, notified federal law enforcement and brought in outside responders. It believes the attacker obtained the name, contact information, payment card number and card expiration date of customers who saved payment details in their online shopping account. The Record reports Texas breach filings list 127,200 affected Texans, plus 1,328 in Vermont and a Massachusetts notice; no total was given. No attacker, start date or entry point disclosed, and no claim seen on leak sites. Revolve says there was no operational disruption and it does not expect a material impact. If you shop there, watch card statements and consider replacing a saved card. (The Record's headline spells the name "Resolve"; the filing and article body say Revolve.)
