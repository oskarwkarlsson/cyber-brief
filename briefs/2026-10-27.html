KEV, exploited

PHP2Shell is now on CISA's KEV list; federal agencies must patch by Oct 29

CISA added CVE-2026-109618 (memory corruption) and CVE-2026-109619 (stale request state) on Oct 26 after confirming active exploitation, and updated its PHP alert. SecurityWeek reports attackers launched 35 working shells against honeypots from two IP addresses on Oct 24, after days of probing. Patch to PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35, restart workers, and on Windows take the rebuilt -1 packages that also fix the Brotli bug.

Healthcare

Cyber incident shuts University of New Mexico Health's main hospital to new patients and cancels clinic appointments

UNM Health, which runs the state's only Level 1 trauma center, diverted ambulances on Monday and cancelled appointments at its 30+ clinics; it says hospitals and clinics began reopening in phases later that day. Staff told the AP that messages appeared on their computers claiming student, employee and patient data had been stolen. The FBI is assisting; no group has claimed it and the cause is not confirmed.

AI tenant isolation

Cloudflare: Workers AI's GLM-5.3 returned other customers' data in 155 responses

Between Aug 27 and Sept 9, prompts longer than 681,280 tokens to @cf/zai-org/glm-5.3 could receive text from other users' recent requests. A context window bigger than the KV cache pool hit an SGLang bug in disaggregated prefill/decode mode, so decode attended over stale cache pages. Knostic's Gadi Evron reported it; Cloudflare pulled the model within 40 minutes, fixed it the same day and found no sign of deliberate exploitation. If you self-host SGLang in that mode, check pool size against context length and update.

Linux LPE

Public root exploit for a Linux kernel networking bug, still without a CVE or merged fix in Monday's reports

ZDI-CAN-33367 lets a local user change the in-memory copy of read-only files such as setuid binaries or /etc/passwd. CloudLinux says it got root on AlmaLinux 8 to 10, CloudLinux 9 and 10, Debian 12 and 13 and Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04. It needs local access, so shared hosting, CI runners and multi-tenant containers matter most; watch your distro's tracker for a fix.
