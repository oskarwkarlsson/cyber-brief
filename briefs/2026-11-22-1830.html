Cleverbridge breach: stored payment card data copied

Cleverbridge, a merchant-of-record platform that sells software and subscriptions for vendors (it lists Red Hat, SUSE, Tenable, Veeam, Jamf, Sony and Parallels as customers), says it detected unauthorized activity on Nov 4 and later found that a third party copied payment card data stored on its platform. It began notifying affected people on Nov 20 and says it was not ransomware. A notice seen by BleepingComputer lists card number, expiry date and CVV/CVC; another notice shared online lists card number, expiry and contact details but says CVV was not copied, so the exposed fields seem to vary. Cleverbridge has not said how the attacker got in, how long they had access, how many cards are affected, or which vendors' buyers were hit. If you bought software through a Cleverbridge checkout, watch card statements, consider asking your issuer to replace the card, and expect follow-up phishing that uses the breach as a lure.
