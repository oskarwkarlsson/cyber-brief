SSHole (CVE-2026-123158): OpenSSH musl bypass now under active exploitation

Friday's daily had no exploitation reported yet. Within hours that changed: Previdian says the first attempt came less than nine hours after the CVE published and, per The Hacker News, it confirmed root on an Alpine Linux OpenSSH honeypot. Previdian's CVE page, updated 8 Nov, now counts 19 exploitation attempts from 12 attacker IPs in 8 countries across 4 sensors, first seen 6 Nov and last seen 7 Nov (it notes attempts do not prove the targets were vulnerable). The bug (CVSS 8.1) hits OpenSSH on musl libc with PAM off, such as Alpine, OpenWrt, Buildroot and Yocto: a 257 to 1,024 byte password logs into accounts whose hash is "*", if password logins are on and another account has a real hash. Alpine says every release it checked, 3.16 through 3.24 and edge, is affected. Glibc and PAM setups are out of scope. Takeaway: patch to 10.6p2 or your distro build and restart sshd, or set PasswordAuthentication no, and check Alpine sshd logs for "Accepted password for" on key-only accounts.

CISA KEV: SonicWall SonicOS SSL-VPN SAML/MFA bypass (CVE-2026-19868) exploited

CISA added CVE-2026-19868 to KEV on 6 Nov, with a federal deadline of 20 Nov. SonicOS SSL-VPN configured for SAML can let someone holding valid credentials for an alternate authentication method bypass SAML controls, potentially including SAML-enforced MFA (CVSS 7.1). SonicWall says it investigated a report of potential exploitation and has not shared details; Ryan Harris of The Electronic Office found the flaw. The same advisory (SNWLID-2026-0014) fixes six more SonicOS bugs, including two 8.8-rated management-interface flaws that let unauthenticated attackers perform unauthorized operations under specific conditions, plus pre-auth SSL-VPN and TACACS+ overflows that can cause denial of service. Known ransomware use: unknown. Takeaway: upgrade SonicOS now if SSL-VPN uses SAML, and keep firewall management interfaces off the internet.

Storm-3358 adopts ARTEX agents against banks and other financial firms

Microsoft Threat Intelligence says the financially motivated, Cambodia-based cluster Storm-3358 began using the agentic AI offensive framework ARTEX in October 2026, mainly against financial organizations in Southeast Asia plus a smaller number of large financial firms globally. ARTEX agents and conventional automated tools scan internet-facing systems, exploit flaws such as anonymous file upload and SSRF, then pull open-source RATs (WISP, vshell, AdaptixC2) with netcat and curl for persistence and lateral movement. Microsoft published IOCs (active as of 6 Nov), Defender detections and hunting queries, and recommends honeypots, tarpits and "fake wins" to divert autonomous agents. It is the first named criminal crew on ARTEX after earlier nation-state and secret-hunting reports, and lands the same week Check Point counted 1,608 exposed ARTEX panels. Single primary source; Microsoft's IOC table lists 178.16.54[.]129 while its Sentinel query lists 178.15.54.129, so check both. Takeaway: in finance, sweep the IOCs, watch for machine-speed scanning bursts with default tool user agents, and harden upload and SSRF-prone web apps.

QNAP: Plex2Shell is compromising customer NAS devices

QNAP says it has reports that Plex Media Server on customer NAS boxes appears compromised through the Plex2Shell chain (CVE-2026-96652, -96654, -96655, -96656), and support is helping affected users. Plex has confirmed widespread exploitation of internet-facing servers since a public PoC on 16 Oct, and CISA put all four on KEV on 20 Oct; compromised servers have run cryptocurrency miners and proxy software. The flaws are in Plex, not QTS or QuTS hero. Fixed Plex 1.43.3.10861 is in the App Center, but QNAP says that if Plex was internet-reachable on 1.43.2 or earlier, updating alone will not clear payloads: treat the NAS as compromised, clean-reinstall the OS and reset Plex and NAS credentials. Takeaway: update Plex, close port 32400 / Remote Access unless it sits behind a VPN, and reinstall any box that was exposed.
