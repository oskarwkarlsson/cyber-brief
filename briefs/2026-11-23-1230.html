Idunno.AntiCsrf CVE-2026-131914 exploited, no patch coming

Mandiant's advisory MNDT-2026-0048 (published Nov 18) says the legacy ASP.NET WebForms module Idunno.AntiCsrf (0.9.1.0 / 0.9.1 Beta, last released December 2008 on CodePlex) deserializes the CSRF form token with an unkeyed ObjectStateFormatter. Any non-empty CSRF cookie plus a Base64 gadget chain in the form field gives code execution as w3wp.exe, with no login and no valid token, even on pages without a server form. CVSS 4.0 score 9.3, and Mandiant says active exploitation has been observed; it found the bug during an incident response. The project is defunct, so the only fix is removal: grep Web.config and bin/ for Idunno.AntiCsrf or AntiCsrfModule (hashes are in the advisory), replace it with AntiForgery or ViewStateUserKey, check IIS logs for odd POSTs to .aspx returning 302 or 500, and rotate machineKey and other Web.config secrets if hit. Not in CISA KEV yet.

CERT-In: AI-assisted payment fraud spreads beyond banks

A CERT-In advisory dated Nov 20 says attackers have caused unauthorized transactions at financial firms and also at technology providers supporting government-linked payments and at vendor or partner payout platforms. Instead of hitting payment APIs directly, they change business records, beneficiaries or approval relationships and then let legitimate payment processes do the rest, exploiting weak reconciliation and change review. CERT-In separately reports AI-driven agentic offensive frameworks used against enterprise, government, academic and research targets for automated recon, credential attacks, parallel multi-agent intrusion and fast exploit generation from patches, saying agentic tooling cut access-to-impact from days to hours in the Indian financial campaign. No amounts, victims or attribution given. This widens the target set of the AI-assisted campaign CERT-In earlier named CRAFTY FALCON.
