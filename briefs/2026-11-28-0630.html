Cendyn eInsight CRM breach exposes hotel guest bookings; targeted phishing follows

Palace Hotel Tokyo and Zentis Osaka say Cendyn told them on Nov 26 that an unknown party accessed its eInsight CRM between Nov 20 and 23, affecting "numerous" client hotels: 14,304 stays at Palace Hotel Tokyo and up to 8,163 bookings at Zentis Osaka, for stays from Nov 20 on. Exposed: names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, confirmation numbers, dates, room types and rates, and for some, birth dates and nationality. Cendyn says no card data, passwords or passport/ID numbers. Hotels warn of emails, texts and WhatsApp messages posing as them to ask for payment. Separately, Troy Hunt reports very targeted phishing quoting his family's Rosewood Hotels stay, dates and phone number; a link to Cendyn is not confirmed. Cendyn itself has not published a statement we could find. Takeaway: treat any hotel message asking for payment or card re-entry as phishing and call the hotel directly.
