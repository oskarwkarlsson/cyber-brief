Mercado Bitcoin drained of about $49 million across 17 blockchains; nearly $10 million frozen

Mercado Bitcoin, which calls itself Latin America's largest digital assets platform, froze deposits and withdrawals on Monday after confirming a cyberattack. Chainalysis says an attacker moved assets in waves across 17 chains in the early hours of Nov 2, mostly Bitcoin and Ether, plus Base, Tron and Polygon, for an estimated $49 million. Working with law enforcement, Tether, Circle, Paxos, Ripple and Anchorage, it helped freeze nearly $10 million, including over $9 million in USDT. Mercado Bitcoin says customer funds were not accessed; several blockchain security experts dispute that, pointing to funds siphoned from some customer wallets. No attribution yet. First flagged in the 00:30 alert.

Vulns

Synology warns of a surge in C3RB3R ransomware hitting NAS devices on old DSM versions

In a rare advisory, its first such warning since 2019, Synology says attackers are exploiting a vulnerability patched in December 2024 to encrypt data on outdated DiskStation Manager installs. It has not named the bug, but links its November 2024 advisory covering CVE-2024-10441 (code execution), CVE-2024-50629 (file read) and CVE-2024-10445 (file write). Victims see a read-me3.txt ransom note and a .L0CK3D file extension. Shadowserver sees over 380,000 Synology NAS devices exposed online. Update DSM (fixed in 7.2.2-72806-1, 7.2.1-69057-6, 7.2-64570-4, 7.1.1-42962-7 and 6.2.4-25556-8) and block the management interface from the internet.

AI/Cloud

Aur0ra ransomware gang handed AI coding agents VPN logins and let them work up to domain admin

Sophos says GOLD OGRE, the group behind Aur0ra ransomware, used Cursor's Composer agent plus models from Anthropic, xAI and Moonshot to run intrusions. Operators supplied VPN credentials, SOCKS access, targets and goals; the agents did AD discovery, Kerberoasting, NTLM relay and lateral movement, with prompts written in Russian. In some networks they reached domain management access, and three of those victims later showed up on Aur0ra's leak site. Guardrails were bypassed by calling the work authorized red teaming or "diagnostics testing". Deploying the ransomware stayed human. It lands the same day CrowdStrike put China's ARTEX-driven Vapor Panda campaign at 200 organizations.

Incidents

Recap: second wave of supermarket freezer hacks hits Sweden, Belgium and four more countries

In the early hours of Monday attackers remotely raised temperatures on store refrigeration systems, with freezers reportedly set to 50°C, spoiling food in Sweden, more than 50 stores in Belgium, and in Ireland, Estonia, the Netherlands and Luxembourg. It follows October's wave in France. No claim of responsibility so far. CERT-SE is in contact with affected Swedish businesses. First flagged in the 18:30 alert.
