Actors

GT13 owns Check Point management servers at 22 orgs

GreyNoise tracks an actor it calls GT13 that compromised 24 Check Point Security Management servers at 22 organizations in 17 countries (government, finance, energy, transport, mining, manufacturing), seen Oct 17 to Nov 2. It could not tell how the servers were first breached, but saw post-compromise use of SmartConsole auth bypass CVE-2026-16232 plus attempts on Citrix, Ivanti, NetLogon and Linux bugs, with a hijacked Rejetto HFS server (CVE-2026-61500) as relay. GreyNoise believes AI agentic tooling drove it, but says AI use is unconfirmed. Likely still active; IoCs published.

VMware: 9.8 vCenter auth bypass, critical ESX bugs

Broadcom patched 23 flaws across VMware products. vCenter gets CVE-2026-123903 (CVSS 9.8), an authentication bypass in the VMware Directory Service, plus six more. ESX gets CVE-2026-123910 and CVE-2026-123911 (9.8, I/O filter heap overflow and use-after-free) and CVE-2026-123912 (9.3, XHCI USB, needs VM admin), giving code execution as the VMX process; contained to the sandbox on ESX but reaching the host on Workstation and Fusion. No exploitation reported, but it lands days after the exploited ARMORY CASTLE vCenter zero-day. Patch now.

European Commission confirms EU Academy data theft

Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier confirmed attackers also breached the EU Academy learning platform and leaked users' names and email addresses (number undisclosed), after last week's disclosure of the Oct 22 ARTEX AI-assisted intrusion that disrupted the Sysper HR system. Public access to EU Academy is disabled and users are warned about follow-on phishing. No ransom demand and no attribution yet; this upgrades the earlier "no evidence of a breach" line.

Volant Manticore: MuddyWater offshoot with IPFS C2

Check Point Research describes a previously undocumented Iran-aligned espionage group, active since late August 2025, that it assesses as former MuddyWater (MOIS) operators now working separately. It hit telecoms, airlines and critical infrastructure in Cyprus, including an airline flying Cyprus to Israel, using AI-generated personas in fake CVs to drop KuboShell, a backdoor with IPFS command and control, then the Homa framework and RMM tools (MeshCentral, NetLock). Targeting has shifted toward Saudi healthcare.
