AI/Cloud

Meta Muse agent hijacked end to end via one webpage

NebuSec (write-ups published Nov 20) shows that when Muse saved an attacker-controlled page as a PDF, a public n-day V8 bug in Muse's bundled, lagging Chromium (IPC sandbox disabled for PDF export) gave code execution inside the agent's runtime container. The researchers then bypassed the Sentinel egress policy to pull chat history and memories to their server with no approval prompt, and chained a shared Ubuntu kernel flaw to get root in the user's VM, reaching the credential store and Sentinel itself. It stayed within one user's VM (no host or cross-tenant escape). Lab demo, not an attack on real users; NebuSec says everything was fixed before publication. Meta has not publicly confirmed. Lesson for any agent stack: the browser an agent drives is the soft entry point.

BADBOX 3.0: record ad fraud plus proxyjacking from preinstalled TV-box malware

HUMAN's Satori team says it disrupted BADBOX 3.0, which peaked at about 40 billion fraudulent bid requests a day (over 4% of the connected-TV traffic HUMAN sees, three times the previous record). Uncertified AOSP streaming boxes built in mainland China ship with backdoors that pull hidden-ad and proxy modules from public repos and mirrors, and enrol owners' home connections in 11 residential proxy networks, including PXYEDGE (assessed successor to IpMoYu; about 200,000 advertised nodes). HUMAN links the catalog to Chinese manufacturer TXCZ and took part in Google's takedown of related XCPE C2 domains this month. Announced Nov 18; widely reported today. A box that streams normally can still be infected.
