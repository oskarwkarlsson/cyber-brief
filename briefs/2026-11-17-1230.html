Optimizely: a platform vulnerability, now fixed, let an attacker inject JavaScript into customer projects

Optimizely's status page (posted 02:02 CET, after this morning's daily was researched) says an unauthorized party "exploited a vulnerability in Experimentation" to add malicious JavaScript to some customer projects; the flaw is fixed, injected code removed, affected accounts secured and new protections added. That answers yesterday's open question: this was a vendor-side flaw, so any Web Experimentation customer could have been exposed, not only those with leaked credentials. Optimizely has not said how many customers were hit or published a root cause; Report URI counted 188 infected scripts across 178 organisations. Hudson Rock adds that some poisoned scripts carried the full payload inline, so blocking the attacker domain alone may not stop it, and says links to iOS exploit-kit infrastructure are confirmed but successful device exploitation is not. Takeaway: Optimizely Web users should still audit Project JS and project change logs, purge caches and rotate tokens; watch for a proper RCA.

Alleged "8-Digits Team" ransomware member extradited from Italy to the US

The DOJ says Roman Khlynovskiy, 43, a Kazakh national, appeared in federal court in Knoxville, Tennessee on 16 November after extradition from Italy, charged over a ransomware and extortion conspiracy against US schools, hospitals and healthcare entities in at least six states. Prosecutors say the group boasted of 50+ TB of data including over 70 million health records and made over $500 million in ransom demands. BleepingComputer ties the 8-Digits Team to the INC and Lynx ransomware operations; he faces up to 27 years if convicted. Takeaway: another affiliate arrest in the INC/Lynx ecosystem; the brand itself remains active.
