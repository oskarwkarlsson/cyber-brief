CISA confirms GeoServer and Drupal installer bugs are exploited with a Nov 30 deadline, pCloud contains an attack on its Pass password manager, and Boston Scientific says a vishing call exposed data on 117,789 people

Top stories
Vulns

CISA adds GeoServer CVE-2026-120705, CVE-2026-129243 and Drupal CVE-2026-136716 to KEV

Added Nov 27 with a federal deadline of Monday Nov 30. The GeoServer pair (unauthenticated XML injection for file reads and SSRF, plus a service-level auth bypass) are parts of a public chain to unauthenticated RCE; on Nov 25 only probes were reported. The Drupal entry is the installer gap left by last week's fix, and CISA flags it for forensic triage. Takeaway: patch GeoServer per its Nov 23 advisory and apply SA-CORE-2026-019, then check Drupal sites for takeover.

Incidents

pCloud confirms an attack on pCloud Pass after users saw unknown "pd" device logins

pCloud says the attack was contained, a small number of user emails were exposed and login attempts were made; it says vault data is end-to-end encrypted and storage was unaffected, but has not said whether encrypted vaults were taken. Some alerts hit 2FA users and people who never used Pass. Takeaway: Pass users should make sure the master password is strong and unique, and expect phishing.

Incidents

Boston Scientific: vishing call opened its cloud apps, 117,789 people affected

Breach notices say a voice-phishing attacker reached cloud-hosted apps and data in May; exposed data includes names, addresses, SSNs, government IDs, medical and insurance details. The company says device and remote-monitoring data were not exposed and this is unrelated to its August intrusion. Takeaway: another help-desk/vishing route into SaaS; check caller verification for access resets.
