Vulns

N-able Mail Assure CVE-2026-134624 now in CISA KEV

CISA added the Mail Assure / SpamExperts SSRF (CVSS 9.3) to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Nov 23 with a Nov 26 due date and a forensic triage requirement. That upgrades last evening's story: N-able had patched the flaw (version 20261121.1943) after finding it during its breach investigation, but had not said it was exploited. The bug lets an authenticated API user make the LDAP sync check connect to arbitrary internal hosts and ports and send crafted data. N-able hosts the service; customers should rotate Mail Assure API credentials and review relay and LDAP settings, and watch for the root cause analysis promised within two weeks.

MONAI Label CVE-2026-133968: in KEV, fix out in 0.8.6

CISA also added the MONAI Label path traversal that Amazon says Chinese operators' Cairn AI agents found and used for root at East Asian universities: unauthenticated arbitrary file write outside the datastore, potentially RCE, due Nov 26. Earlier editions said no fixed release existed; the project's advisory now lists 0.8.6 as patched (versions up to 0.8.5 vulnerable), and 0.8.6 was published on GitHub on Nov 22. Upgrade, and keep MONAI Label servers off the internet.

LaLiga confirms fan data theft

Spain's top football league says an attacker accessed one of its fan services platforms between Nov 14 and 16 and extracted personal data of users registered for digital services from LaLiga and some clubs. It blocked the access on Nov 16, says competition systems were not affected, reported to the AEPD and the National Police, and is notifying clubs and fans. Which clubs and which data fields are not disclosed, and no group has claimed it. Expect football-themed phishing.
