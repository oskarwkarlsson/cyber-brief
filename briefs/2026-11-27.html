Drupal installer fix bypassed, exploited

Drupal SA-CORE-2026-019 (CVE-2026-136716, "highly critical") says sites left in unusual or incomplete install states stayed open to takeover after the Nov 18 fix for KEV-listed CVE-2026-132165, and it is exploited in the wild. The state is invisible in the UI. Fixed in 11.4.14, 11.3.23 and 10.6.23 (then run update.php); 11.0 to 11.2 and 10.5 and older get no fix. Takeaway: upgrade, block install.php and check logs since Nov 18.

Todoist breached via stolen dev credential

On Nov 22 and 23 an attacker used a stolen version-control credential to alter workflows in three repos, harvest more credentials, take over an employee account and reach cloud infrastructure. Taken: emails, salted SHA1 hashes and creation dates for about 6% of users, API and Slack tokens for about 0.003%, plus email data via Todoist for Outlook. It is the second dev-credential-to-CI/CD breach in two days after 1Password. Takeaway: change reused passwords and reconnect integrations.

Vectors

Mail theft moves into the browser

Bitdefender says the new SYS010 stealer, pushed through hundreds of Meta ads for fake wallpaper apps, enrols Chrome in an attacker-run Google admin console (or the PC in an attacker MDM) to force unremovable extensions that steal session cookies and read Gmail and Outlook. Separately, GenDigital found the Remus seller's "Gmail zero-day" is a fake "Google Sheets" extension side-loaded via Developer Mode, which survives password resets. Takeaway: check chrome://management and chrome://extensions on unmanaged PCs.

Actors

"GoodGuys" paid-interview phishing

Push Security has tracked since Oct 13 lures offering up to $600 for "expert interviews" while impersonating Accenture, Amex, Google Cloud, Nvidia, SpaceX and others, sent via HireVue, SendGrid and Cvent. Fake Google and Okta logins take corporate accounts only and operators appear to relay any MFA challenge, including Okta Verify push. Takeaway: phishing-resistant MFA blunts this; warn staff about paid interview invites asking for a work login.
